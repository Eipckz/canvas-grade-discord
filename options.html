CANVAS TO DISCORD

Your grades, delivered.

UTC Canvas and the shared Discord destination are already connected. This extension uses your signed-in browser session and sends class names, current grades, assignment scores, and grading feedback to your webhook.

Canvas: https://utchattanooga.instructure.com/

Schedule: One report daily at 6 PM Eastern, with catch-up when your browser starts or Canvas loads. Reports send even when grades have not changed.

Reports include the signed-in person's name and grades and go to the preconfigured shared Discord channel. The destination and automatic delivery are locked in this extension's settings.

Your browser must be running and Canvas signed in. A red badge means the connection or delivery needs attention.
